01

The house

Two brothers, one ancestral home.

Cascade Hotel stands where the founders' family home once stood — a house where guests, often from other countries, were always received with generosity. The two brothers who built the hotel inherited that tradition, and the two hands in the hotel's emblem stand for their shared work.

A clock and a piano from the old house were brought into the sitting room, which is arranged like the living room of a home. The first impression is meant to be of a fine house rather than a hotel.

The building sits just off the Cascade, away from the noise of the centre: marble, fine woods and modern furniture inside, and a wide view over the city outside.
